Reproduced on the TV emulator (x86_64 Google TV image) with a build of current `main`: direct play of a 4K HEVC Main 10 Dolby Vision remux (about 98 Mbps) fails in both emulator decoders (`c2.goldfish.hevc.decoder`, then `c2.android.hevc.decoder`) with `format_supported=NO_EXCEEDS_CAPABILITIES`, so the title never starts: the emulator's decoders cannot take 4K HEVC at that bitrate (documented emulator caveat; a real device with a hardware decoder plays it). PR 163 does fall back: after the decoder retries the app renegotiates a 1080p H.264 transcode at the same position (`reprepare reason=replace_item`). Second root cause found there: the transcode ran on a peer node and the negotiation answer was a `Redirect` URL on that peer. An HLS manifest names its segments relatively and the client sends its bearer token only to its own server origin, so the peer answered 401 (server log: `GET /api/v1/media/sessions/{session_id}/{file_name}` status 401 in 0.05 ms; the app showed "io bad http status"). Direct-play URLs carry `playback_session_id` themselves and were unaffected. Fix (merged): `rewrite_for_delivery` now proxies HLS even when `Redirect` was chosen (the proxy rewrites the manifest to carry the capability); direct play still redirects. Tests: `delegated_hls_is_proxied_even_when_redirect_was_chosen`, `delegated_direct_play_is_still_redirected`; `playarr-api` suite 498 passed, clippy clean.
