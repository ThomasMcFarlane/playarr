- Android: minimising a video now uses system Picture-in-Picture (also entered automatically when leaving the app during playback, on API 31+ via auto-enter and on older versions via the user-leave hint). The window takes its aspect ratio from the video, offers play/pause and 10 second skip actions, hides all controls, and closing it stops playback and records progress. PiP is gated on the device feature, so devices without it simply do not offer minimise for video; the in-app mini player remains for music only.
