Playarr is a native iPhone, iPad and Apple TV client for a Playarr Server operated by you, your household or your organisation.

Use Playarr to:
• Connect to a Playarr Server using its HTTPS address, or sign in on Apple TV with a short code approved from a phone or computer.
• Browse films, television series, music, books and playlists.
• Search the catalogue and view titles, seasons, episodes and media details.
• Stream supported video and audio with the native Apple player, including direct play and HLS.
• Resume where you left off with server-synchronised watch progress.
• Download permitted media for offline playback on iPhone and iPad.
• Switch between profiles managed by your Playarr Server.
• Send playback to a Chromecast on your home network.

Playarr does not provide or sell media, streaming subscriptions, cloud storage, user accounts or a public catalogue. You need access to a reachable Playarr Server. The person or organisation operating that server supplies and controls access. Available libraries and features depend on the server configuration and the access granted by its operator.

Playarr collects no data, shows no advertising and contains no tracking. Questions: support@playarr.app
